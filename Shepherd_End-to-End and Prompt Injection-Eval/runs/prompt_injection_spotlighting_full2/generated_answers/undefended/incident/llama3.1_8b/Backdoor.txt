Given an IoT security incident where Backdoor is the dominant attack, what immediate containment actions should the administrator take first?

Reference Answer:
The administrator should identify the affected endpoint or endpoints and confirm their operational role, then move affected endpoints into a quarantine VLAN or restricted network segment. Suspicious inbound and outbound connections should be blocked at the firewall, router, access control list, or software-defined network policy layer. Exposed management services that are not required for investigation should be disabled, and any necessary remote administration should be restricted to a trusted management workstation. Logs, configuration snapshots, packet captures, device state, and file hashes should be preserved before major remediation changes.
